## WP-62 — Site nav flyouts: continuous hit area and hover intent (2026-10-25)
**What:** The desktop nav dropdowns no longer vanish while the pointer travels to them. **Root cause:** The header wrapper in `site-header.tsx` is `pointer-events-none`, with only links, buttons and `[role=menu]` turned back on. So the flyout `li` was itself dead space, and the only hoverable part of a trigger was its 13px `leading-none` text. `onMouseLeave` also closed the menu instantly. **Fix:** Each flyout `li` is now `pointer-events-auto` and padded by `-mx-2 -my-3 px-2 py-3`, so the layout doesn't move. The panel sits at `top-full` of that padded box, so the trigger and the panel form one continuous hit area. Menu rows are full-width blocks with `px-5 py-2`. The new `useHoverIntent` hook owns the open state. Opening waits 100ms, so sweeping across the bar doesn't flash menus. Closing waits 300ms, and re-entering the trigger or the panel cancels it. While a menu is open, moving to the next trigger swaps instantly. All timers are refs, and they are cleared on unmount. Hover only reacts to mouse and pen. On touch, the first tap on a trigger opens its menu, and a second tap follows the link. For the keyboard: focus opens the menu. ArrowDown or ArrowUp opens it and focuses the first or last item. Arrow keys, Home and End move through the items. Escape closes the menu and returns focus to its trigger. Moving focus out of the item closes it, but only its own menu. `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and `aria-haspopup` are unchanged. The panel has no animation, so reduced motion needs no handling. The nav items and header styling are unchanged. **Files:** `components/layout/site-nav/{site-nav.tsx,use-hover-intent.ts,use-hover-intent.test.ts}`. **Verified:** storefront typecheck (clean); eslint on `site-nav` (0 problems); vitest `site-nav` 8/8, with 6 new hover-intent tests. In the browser on `/about`, a hit-test along three vertical lines from 8px above CATALOG down to its last item found 0 points outside the flyout. The menu was closed at 50ms and open at 150ms, still open 200ms after leaving, and closed by 350ms. Re-entry kept it open, and CATALOG → INFO swapped instantly.

## WP-53 — Category furniture: smooth swaps, full 360° spin, no clipping (2026-10-25)
**What:** Builds on WP-52. Each slot now keeps a short list of pieces. On a category change, the outgoing piece stays mounted and sinks out in 0.42s, shrinking, dropping away from the scroll direction, and still turning. The incoming piece eases in over 0.8s, starting 0.16s later with a 0.07s stagger per slot. Both use one ease-out-cubic curve. Fast hover sweeps never stack more than three pieces in a slot. A piece that is still leaving when its category comes back reverses in place. The idle motion is now a continuous 360° turntable, one revolution every 15s, offset per slot. Reduced motion stays static and swaps instantly. No piece is cut by its slot at any angle. Each model's spin radius and height are measured once from its vertices. The scale is then binary-searched per kind and slot aspect, so that the spin cylinder projects inside 90% of the slot's frustum. Pieces scale about their centre, and the enter/exit drop is 35% of the fitted half-height, so in-between frames stay inside the fit too. The contact shadow is a circle of 0.9 × the spin radius. Wide tables come out smaller than tall pieces, as expected. The fixed per-kind `scale`/`height`/`footprint` values are gone, and `FURNITURE` is now kind → model. **Optimisation:** Textures, materials, and geometries live in a module cache keyed by their parameters. They are built once and disposed only when the canvas unmounts. Before, each armchair swap redrew the bouclé pair, about 170ms of main-thread work. The lightformers are captured once into a shared cube map, so there is one capture and one PMREM pass instead of one per view. After mount, the six models are built and their shaders compiled one per idle callback, behind the camera, with `compileAsync`, and their textures are uploaded then. `dpr` is capped at 1.5 instead of 2. The models' geometry and materials, the lighting, the fixed canvas, the offscreen pause, and the materials section are unchanged. **Why:** The user asked for a smoother, more optimised transition, faster rotation, a complete 360° turn, and no pieces cut off by their slots. **Files:** `components/sections/products-categories/category-canvas.tsx`, `furniture-models.tsx`. **Verified:** storefront typecheck, eslint on `products-categories`, `materials`, and `lib/three`, and the `products-categories` vitest run all pass. In the browser, no long tasks (>50ms) appeared across eight switches, and rapid sweeps leave no stray pieces. A headless Chromium check covered all five categories at four quarter-turn angles, at 1440px and 390px, and found 0 model pixels on any slot edge. → [L3/041](L3/041-category-furniture-transitions.md)
