## 8. Materials

- Eyebrow: `//materials`
- Headline: We name what we use.
- Optical crystal: Lead-free, polished to a true edge. Takes an engraving without clouding.
- Cast brass: Sand-cast and hand-buffed. It darkens with handling, which is the point.
- Solid walnut: Quarter-sawn for stability. Grain matched across a single piece, never veneered.
- Kiln-dried teak: Dried to ten percent moisture, so a Bengaluru monsoon does not move it.
- Full-grain leather: Vegetable-tanned. It creases where you sit and nowhere else.
- Honed stone: Matte, not polished. Fingerprints do not live on it.
